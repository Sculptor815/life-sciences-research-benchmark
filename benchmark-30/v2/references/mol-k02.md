# mol-k02: revised reference

Version: reference-v2.1. All procedures and numerical pilot criteria are proposed, not claims of historical execution. New revisions await owner review. Scientifically equivalent alternative designs can satisfy the rubric.

Observed evidence: the figure presents quantified band intensities from a synthetic knockout validation experiment, not raw blot images. The 50-kDa band falls in the knockout, while the 75-kDa band is unchanged. The 50-kDa band therefore has the stronger genetic specificity support. The alternative claim assigns that support to the 75-kDa band, but a signal that does not change in the supplied validated knockout cannot be said to depend on the target locus; it remains unassigned. The claim that both bands are equally specific, fails for the same reason, because the genotype-specific behavior differs between the two bands. The claim that neither band can ever be assessed by a genetic control, overstates the limitation. Genetic loss is a recognized specificity criterion: if a validated gene disruption removes a band, that band is supported as target-derived in that assay. The support is real but bounded, because knockout validation supports specificity rather than proving it universally, which is why stronger genetic specificity support is preferable wording to proven specific. Several caveats follow from the figure being quantified intensities rather than blots. Saturation, uneven transfer, membrane stripping and antibody concentration cannot be judged from summary bars, so a partial rather than complete reduction of the 50-kDa signal would be hard to distinguish from incomplete knockout or from a mixture of target and cross-reactive signal at the same size. If both bands were quantified on the same membrane and the 75-kDa signal is unchanged, that pattern is consistent with a cross-reacting protein sharing an epitope, a distinct gene product, or a modified species, and this figure does not separate those alternatives. The origin note says the figure is an original synthetic teaching plot generated deterministically, so the values are illustrative rather than a real experiment. Uncertainty therefore includes both biological ambiguity about the 75-kDa species and measurement ambiguity about how the bars were produced. Useful discriminating tests, proposed rather than performed, include an antibody against a different, non-overlapping epitope; immunogen peptide competition; an independent second loss-of-function reagent such as another deletion allele or an inducible degron; epitope-tagged target expression to identify the cognate band; mass spectrometry of the excised 75-kDa band; and target-null cells. The predictions differ: a genuine target species should disappear with independent target-specific perturbations, while a cross-reactive band should persist and should gain no support from those perturbations. the 50-kDa band has the stronger genetic specificity support, the 75-kDa band remains unassigned, and quantified intensities cannot substitute for raw images or orthogonal validation.

## Strict second-round operational extension

The supplied figure is a summary of synthetic values: the 50-kDa bar is approximately 100 versus 5 arbitrary units and the 75-kDa bar approximately 95 versus 93. Thus the first band has much stronger genetic-dependence support. No biological replicate count, variance, raw-blot quality or significance test can be inferred from these bars. Unlike mol-k01, this packet does not specify deletion of the entire coding region; do not import that stronger genetic fact into this item.

Before validation, consult the antibody Methods and lot documentation and verify the relevant epitope and genotype. Define the parental/null/rescue comparison, loading/transfer control and no-primary background control before collecting blots. These answer different questions; a loading control is not an antigen-identity control. Request unprocessed blot images, exposure metadata, genotype validation and independent culture identities. Quantify a fixed band window after local-background subtraction and total-protein normalization, reporting the raw intensities and normalized ratios together. Pilot a twofold loading series; freeze an exposure with no clipped pixels and approximately proportional signal over the selected range. A proposed 15% recovery tolerance is a calibration criterion, not a fact about this figure. Weak signals below the validated quantification limit remain censored.

Use a separate-epitope antibody and near-endogenous rescue, with immunocapture/proteomics where feasible. Peptide competition can block cross-reactivity, and a gel slice may contain multiple proteins; neither is identity proof alone. If independent target loss repeatedly reduces the 50-kDa signal and restoration recovers it while direct identification agrees, support strengthens. If an unrelated protein follows the knockout, an indirect dependency remains possible. If the apparent change is created by saturation or background treatment, the original band interpretation weakens. Failure to resolve the 75-kDa species is an informative unresolved outcome, not evidence that it is necessarily a target isoform.
