# inf-r02: revised reference

Version: reference-v2.1. All procedures and numerical pilot criteria are proposed, not claims of historical execution. New revisions await owner review. Scientifically equivalent alternative designs can satisfy the rubric.

The next biological question is whether joint RNA and surface-protein measurements reveal reproducible cell states that one modality alone misses, while distinguishing real discordance from measurement noise. A methodological route is to let the relative contribution of the modalities vary by cell instead of imposing a universal weighting. The competing explanations are complementary biology, technical modality imbalance and batch- or label-driven apparent separation. A visually attractive embedding cannot discriminate them. All computational steps below are proposed; no raw data or original figures have been reproduced in this assessment.

Before analysis, obtain raw reads or verified feature-count matrices, sample metadata, antibody-panel annotations, controls and an independent validation endpoint. Identify exact publication accessions and target panels from primary records rather than inventing file names. Build a manifest of checksums, retrieval locations, genome and feature-reference releases, barcode conventions, donor identity, condition and batch. Preserve immutable inputs and environment specifications. Check whether the same cell barcode truly joins the two modalities and whether antibody features were quantified with the intended reference.

Split discovery and validation by independent donor or preparation before learning any representation. Within discovery data, reserve separate tuning folds. Define biological state labels or external outcomes that were not created from the same marker channels used to claim success. If the only labels were assigned from included proteins, a protein-informed method will receive circular credit. Use held-out marker panels, orthogonal sorting, functional readouts or an independently annotated validation set where feasible. If these are unavailable, report a narrower claim about internal structure rather than verified new cell types.

Audit cells for library complexity, contamination, doublets and modality-specific background using rules fixed from controls and training data. Estimate protein background with appropriate negative controls or empty-droplet information where available, distinguishing ambient signal from true low expression. Record the effect of filtering by donor and condition. Do not discard RNA-protein-discordant cells merely because they contradict a preferred state label. Preserve the distinction between biological absence and technical missingness. A modality with little signal in one cell may still carry meaningful signal in another.

Normalize and transform each modality using a documented method suitable for its count and background properties. Select features and fit dimensional reductions on training data only. Construct modality-specific neighborhoods and estimate how well each modality predicts local cell relationships or the other modality's structure using cross-validated criteria. Turn these estimates into cell-specific relative weights with an explicit normalization rule. Combine neighborhood information into an integrated graph without inserting validation labels. Freeze feature sets, dimensions, neighbor counts and weighting rules before evaluating the holdout.

Compare the adaptive graph against RNA-only, protein-only, fixed-weight concatenation and a reasonable existing integration baseline under comparable tuning budgets. Use donor-held-out discrimination or neighborhood consistency, rare-state recovery and independent biological validation, not only silhouette scores or UMAP appearance. Test robustness to downsampling, antibody background, feature removal, modality corruption and missing modalities. Learning high weights from technical noise is a failure, not evidence that the method discovered a special cell state. Quantify how weights correlate with library size, batch and protein background.

For biological confirmation, select candidate discordant or newly separated populations according to rules fixed before validation. Test held-out markers or independent functional phenotypes in new preparations. Compare within donor and across donors, preserving appropriate experimental units. A reproducible RNA-protein mismatch may reflect timing, protein turnover or trafficking rather than a new lineage. Formulate a discriminating follow-up, such as a validated time-course or perturbation with paired readouts, and specify what would falsify the proposed biological explanation. Do not retrospectively rename any cluster as a novel state simply because it separates.

Archive every command, software version, parameter, seed, split manifest and intermediate artifact. Rebuild the workflow in a fresh environment and compare numerical outputs behind the target plots with tolerances justified for stochastic steps. Record compute resources and failed stages. Use checksums for inputs; use numeric equivalence and biological contrasts for results because plotting metadata can alter file hashes. A missing accession, ambiguous donor mapping, complete batch-condition confounding or absence of independent labels blocks the corresponding claim and should appear as an explicit unresolved requirement.

Prespecify a primary held-out biological validation metric and a minimum useful improvement over unimodal baselines. Estimate uncertainty by independent donor or preparation resampling, keeping every fitted transformation inside training folds. Apply multiplicity control to exploratory cell-state claims. A supportive result is complementary, cell-dependent information that improves external validation across donors and survives technical stress tests. Failure to improve is informative; a method can still produce a smooth plot while adding no biological value. If only the training data improve, the result is consistent with overfitting.

The published historical-target Hao and colleagues continuation develops weighted nearest-neighbor integration with cell-specific modality weights. Shared authors connect it to the original CITE-seq study. A historical hit requires the complementary-state question, adaptive multimodal neighborhood strategy, leakage-free comparisons and a discriminating prediction about biologically validated states. Mentioning WNN, Seurat or the paper title alone does not qualify. A strong alternative integration method can earn scientific-quality points without this particular historical match. No summary here certifies the later article as fraud-free or proves that raw-data reproduction will succeed.

## Strict second-round operational extension

Operational contract for this proposed protocol. Before collecting confirmatory data, consult the relevant primary Methods, supplements and validated assay/tool protocol. Build a transfer table containing each reagent or tool, source/version, source condition, proposed local condition, reason for changing it and pilot result. This is a required future action, not a claim that unavailable historical procedures have been recovered. Unknown identities or settings remain unresolved prerequisites until the table is complete. Do not replace absent sources with invented precision.

Define positive, negative and procedural controls before treatment: specify which comparison establishes technical responsiveness, which estimates background, and which separates the manipulation from its delivery or handling. Name the competing explanation each controls. A positive control's pass threshold is fixed on independent calibration, not on whether the main treatment gives the hoped-for effect. Use the assay-specific controls below; do not demand unrelated PCR, viral or imaging procedures. If genetic material is amplified, record template/amplicon length, validated primers, reagent amounts, polymerase-specific extension logic and sequence identity. If viral delivery is essential, record the actual construct, functional titer, delivered amount, target-cell number or tissue volume, expression interval, distribution and toxicity; vector-genome concentration alone is not a functional infection dose. These details become obligations only for the chosen route.

Use a separate development pilot to estimate nuisance variability and operating ranges. A proposed minimum of three independent preparations is a variance-discovery starting point, not an automatically adequate powered experiment. Choose a smallest meaningful effect and then simulate or calculate the number of independently allocated units required for a stated interval-width or power target, propagating uncertainty in pilot variance. Fix the maximum sample size, intermediate technical checks and stopping rule before confirmation. If resources are insufficient, report the detectable effect and uncertainty instead of counting subsamples as new replication. Randomize independent units within justified day/batch blocks, balance processing order, conceal allocation where possible and keep outcome scoring and data processing blinded. If an element cannot be blinded, document why and substitute automated locked processing or an independent coded assessment.

Keep a registered primary contrast and multiplicity family. Prespecify how censoring, weak signal, missing units and technical losses are handled. Retain raw and normalized measurements, original assigned-unit denominators and every nonresponse. A failed instrument or sample-identity check can invalidate a measurement under a prospective rule; a biologically negative result cannot. Analyze independent-unit summaries or a justified nested model, verify its assumptions and report effect sizes with intervals. Use equivalence bounds only when an adequately precise absence claim is intended. A non-significant comparison is otherwise inconclusive.

Troubleshoot in the order identity and delivery, reference/control performance, measurement dynamic range, then the biological hypothesis. Record each change, its reason and all failed runs. Change only the implicated variable in a bounded pilot or an explicitly designed factorial pilot; freeze the revised procedure and obtain fresh independent confirmation. Do not retune on the confirmatory outcome. Alternative mechanisms and outright failure of the hypothesis are valid results. The audit trail links sample/unit IDs to raw files, calibration records, timestamps, deviations, exact analysis versions, settings and outputs. A second analyst should be able to reconstruct the reported contrast from those records. No part of this reference claims an experiment has already succeeded.

Case-specific implementation.

Read the original joint-measurement Methods and the integration method's algorithm before specifying normalization and neighborhood parameters. Identify an actual paired RNA/antibody-count dataset with donor/batch metadata, antibody controls, raw provenance and an independent biological endpoint. Verify barcode pairing, feature reference and antibody-background controls. Split by independent donor/preparation before learning features or tuning; reserve the validation endpoint without deriving its labels from proteins used by the model.

Build separate transformed RNA and protein representations with documented likelihood/background assumptions. A proposed implementation learns modality-specific nearest-neighbor structure and cell-specific weights from the relative local predictive utility of the modalities, then combines modality information into a weighted neighborhood graph. Record the exact weighting formula or selected implementation/version; do not describe a global equal average as cell-specific weighting. Explore a bounded development grid such as 10/20/30 dimensions and 10/20/40 neighbors, constrained by available features and cells. Use donor-grouped tuning and freeze the chosen dimensions, normalization, distance and weights before validation. UMAP is a display, not the validation criterion.

Compare RNA-only, protein-only, fixed-weight and adaptive integration under equal tuning budgets. Prespecify a held-out biological discrimination or neighborhood-recovery endpoint, its denominator and uncertainty across donors. Test ambient-protein background, downsampling, modality corruption, missingness, doublets and batch imbalance; do not delete discordant cells merely for disagreeing with a label. Orthogonal confirmation can use a held-out protein panel or prospective functional assay in new preparations, with predefined population selection and independent-unit replication. The same destructive single-cell assay cannot be repeated on the exact same cells simply by preparing another aliquot; aliquots estimate reproducibility at preparation/state levels.

Report RNA-protein discordance as a hypothesis about timing, translation, turnover or trafficking until discriminating evidence exists. An independence-permutation null is not equivalent to a null of no biological discordance. Preserve cell/donor QC and weight distributions, graph outputs, quantitative figure tables, seeds, environment and full command logs. Clean reruns need numeric/stochastic tolerances and a source-difference audit. Improved independent biological validation beyond unimodal/fixed-weight baselines supports complementary adaptive information; a prettier cluster plot or training-only improvement does not. Missing independent endpoints blocks verified-cell-state claims, not permission to invent a successful reproduction.
