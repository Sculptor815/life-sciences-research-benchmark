## Corrections applied before second-round grading

### mol-k01

Previously: Mass spectrometry of the excised 75-kDa band can identify the protein directly.

Revised: Mass spectrometry of the excised 75-kDa region can identify proteins present there, but co-migration does not by itself identify the antibody-bound antigen.

### mol-k01

Previously: if it tracks the target in every orthogonal test, the isoform hypothesis gains support.

Revised: if orthogonal assays seem to implicate the deleted target despite persistent signal in the verified full-coding null, resolve the discordance and possible indirect/co-migrating contributions before assigning an isoform.

### mol-k01

Previously: The residual 75-kDa band is more parsimoniously explained by a cross-reactive protein that shares an epitope, a distinct gene product, an aggregated or modified species, or a degradation fragment.

Revised: The residual 75-kDa signal may arise from another gene product, including a cross-reactive or modified species. A fragment or aggregate newly encoded by the fully deleted locus is not a viable explanation.

### mol-d01

Previously: The independent biological unit is a cell preparation derived from an independent thaw or an independent knockout clone, not a well and not a reading.

Revised: The inferential unit follows independent treatment/manipulation allocation. Preparations, independently generated perturbations, days and any randomized wells have their actual nesting recorded; a well is not an independent K-genotype replicate merely because it was measured separately.

### neu-k01

Previously: Calcium indicators report intracellular free calcium, which is a nonlinear and temporally filtered surrogate for spiking;

Revised: Calcium-sensitive indicators produce an optical response influenced by calcium and the imaging/indicator system, which is a nonlinear and temporally filtered surrogate for spiking;

### neu-k01

Previously: If trial B genuinely contains more spikes with similar timing, a faster indicator and calibrated imaging should show more resolved events and the transfer function should remain in its linear range.

Revised: If paired ground-truth recordings show more spikes in B, calibrated imaging can test whether the optical response agrees within its measured operating range. A faster indicator does not guarantee single-event sensitivity or linearity.

### neu-k01

Previously: If the apparent difference is largely saturation, unequal loading or a few cells with unusually high signal, calibration should reveal a compressed transfer function or unequal effective dye concentration.

Revised: Calibration can reveal compression, unequal loading or baseline history. Saturation alone does not reverse a matched monotonic input-output relation; optical artifact tests remain necessary.

### neu-d01

Previously: if seizures or heating occur, reduce duty cycle and use a red-shifted opsin;

Revised: if harmful effects or heating occur, stop under the approved protocol and redesign/calibrate in a separate pilot; a wavelength change is not itself a guarantee against heating;

### inf-k01

Previously: If the effector is G, colocalization and perturbation of a G-linked regulatory element should track the association.

Revised: An expression-mediated G mechanism predicts informative, context-matched expression/trait and perturbation evidence; an undetected eQTL is not by itself proof against every possible G mechanism.

### inf-p01

Previously: If it disappears when center, ancestry and callable regions are matched, the original signal was likely structural.

Revised: If the estimate materially attenuates after those corrections with adequate precision, a technical explanation gains support. Loss of significance alone may instead reflect reduced sample size or precision.

### bio-k02

Previously: if inhibition is truly substrate-competitive at the active site, direct binding should be displaced by substrate and a catalytic-site mutation should change inhibitor affinity; if the pattern is allosteric or reporter-based, binding may persist without substrate competition, or the apparent shift may change when the coupled reporter is replaced by a direct product measurement.

Revised: substrate competition and carefully validated mutations can constrain binding models, but allosteric coupling can also produce mutual exclusion and mutations need not affect every active-site inhibitor. Reporter replacement tests a different explanation: an apparent shift created by the detection system.

### mol-d02

Previously: The primary quantitative contrast is the damage-induced change in the fraction of live, death-negative cells in G1, and the reciprocal change in nucleotide incorporation, comparing p53-competent with p53-depleted isogenic cultures; the rescue contrast asks whether near-endogenous p53 restores the parental response.

Revised: The primary quantitative contrast is the genotype-by-damage change in longitudinal G1 residence or entry into S phase in the original tracked cohort, with death as a competing event; the rescue contrast asks whether near-endogenous p53 restores the response. Survivor-only G1 fraction and incorporation are complementary summaries with explicit denominators.

### mol-d02

Previously: A secondary contrast is death-adjusted incorporation.

Revised: A secondary summary is incorporation among prospectively defined live singlets, reported together with death and original-cohort counts; this is not an automatic causal adjustment for survival.

### neu-r03

Previously: A fixed-magnitude probability series changes expected value and variance together, so it does not by itself isolate uncertainty.

Revised: A fixed-magnitude probability series changes expected value and variance in related but nonidentical ways: mean is pm and variance is m^2 p(1-p). These functions are not globally linearly collinear. Complementary distributions and behavioral controls strengthen discrimination beyond a fixed-magnitude series.

## Applicability and fairness

Additional reference detail is explanatory, not an unbounded hidden checklist. Only the frozen applicable C/M/G/P criteria can cause deductions. Equivalent scientifically valid designs qualify. Do not double-charge a single omission or require every optional technique. Reference imperfections are not candidate errors; later corrections require an explicit, consistently applied review record.
