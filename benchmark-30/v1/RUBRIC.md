# First-round scoring rubric

Archived original scientific-quality scoring with the owner-requested post-hoc 60/40 research amendment. Candidate answers were read by a single unblinded Codex referee. This version is preserved for comparison.

Each dimension is rated 0 to 4. Q1 = sum(weight x dimension / 4). A scientific-accuracy rating of zero caps Q1 at 20. Refusals receive zero task performance; empty refusals have no explanation score.

| Dimension | Weight | Criterion |
|---|---:|---|
| scientific_accuracy | 35% | Distinguish observations, assumptions and proposals; state correct biological implications and uncertainty without inventing evidence. |
| decision_value | 25% | Resolve the user's stated decision, identify the highest-value uncertainty, prioritize a discriminating next step, and explain what would change the recommendation. |
| actionability | 20% | Provide an ordered feasible plan, prerequisites, controls, decision rules, and explicit blocking inputs; details must support execution rather than merely add length. |
| verifiability | 15% | Trace claims and results to supplied evidence, preserve data/protocol provenance, and specify independent checks; never present a proposed analysis as completed. |
| communication | 5% | Lead with the useful conclusion, separate essential actions from supporting detail, and make limits understandable without verbosity or false confidence. |

## Anchors

- 0: Fundamentally wrong, unsupported or unusable for this criterion; a fatal false premise belongs in scientific accuracy, with the exact contradiction recorded.
- 1: Some relevant content, but the user still lacks the essential information or receives materially misleading guidance.
- 2: Partly useful and scientifically relevant, but substantial verification, redesign or clarification is still needed.
- 3: Reliably advances the stated decision, with a feasible next step and transparent limits; one noncritical issue remains.
- 4: Fully satisfies the stated criterion for this case: precise evidence, justified decisions, practical next steps and explicit conditions that would change the advice.

## Research and category weights

Research item = 60 x qualified historical-direction Hit@1 + 0.40 x Q1. A qualified hit requires the biological question, mechanism, intervention, readout and predicted outcome jointly to match the historical continuation without a critical control or inference failure. Missing matching review is not a miss. Category means are essay 20%, experimental design 30%, research 50%, with ten items per category.

The original reference bank retains item concepts, directed logic chains, operational steps, limitations, service anchors and historical targets. These are now public at the owner's explicit request. No raw candidate responses or private reviewer records are included.

Written explanation is separate: factual correctness, evidence grounding, causal validity, alternatives/limits, each 0 to 4, equally weighted to a 0-100 index. It does not evaluate hidden chain of thought.
