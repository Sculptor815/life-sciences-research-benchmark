# mol-k02: first-round reference

This is the unchanged first-round reference text. The owner confirmed careful review on 3 October 2026. Earlier pending-review statements inside the historical text are retained as part of the snapshot, not current metadata. Corrections and expanded execution requirements appear in version two.

Observed evidence: the figure presents quantified band intensities from a synthetic knockout validation experiment, not raw blot images. The 50-kDa band falls in the knockout, while the 75-kDa band is unchanged. The 50-kDa band therefore has the stronger genetic specificity support. The alternative claim assigns that support to the 75-kDa band, but a signal that does not change in the supplied validated knockout cannot be said to depend on the target locus; it remains unassigned. The claim that both bands are equally specific, fails for the same reason, because the genotype-specific behavior differs between the two bands. The claim that neither band can ever be assessed by a genetic control, overstates the limitation. Genetic loss is a recognized specificity criterion: if a validated gene disruption removes a band, that band is supported as target-derived in that assay. The support is real but bounded, because knockout validation supports specificity rather than proving it universally, which is why stronger genetic specificity support is preferable wording to proven specific. Several caveats follow from the figure being quantified intensities rather than blots. Saturation, uneven transfer, membrane stripping and antibody concentration cannot be judged from summary bars, so a partial rather than complete reduction of the 50-kDa signal would be hard to distinguish from incomplete knockout or from a mixture of target and cross-reactive signal at the same size. If both bands were quantified on the same membrane and the 75-kDa signal is unchanged, that pattern is consistent with a cross-reacting protein sharing an epitope, a distinct gene product, or a modified species, and this figure does not separate those alternatives. The origin note says the figure is an original synthetic teaching plot generated deterministically, so the values are illustrative rather than a real experiment, and the supplied key is a draft awaiting expert review. Uncertainty therefore includes both biological ambiguity about the 75-kDa species and measurement ambiguity about how the bars were produced. Useful discriminating tests, proposed rather than performed, include an antibody against a different, non-overlapping epitope; immunogen peptide competition; an independent second loss-of-function reagent such as another deletion allele or an inducible degron; epitope-tagged target expression to identify the cognate band; mass spectrometry of the excised 75-kDa band; and target-null cells. The predictions differ: a genuine target species should disappear with independent target-specific perturbations, while a cross-reactive band should persist and should gain no support from those perturbations. the 50-kDa band has the stronger genetic specificity support, the 75-kDa band remains unassigned, and quantified intensities cannot substitute for raw images or orthogonal validation.
