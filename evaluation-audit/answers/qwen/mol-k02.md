# Qwen3.8 Max (0902) / mol-k02

Original final answer, shared by both scoring rounds.

**Conclusion.** The 50 kDa band has the stronger genetic-specificity support: its quantified signal is genotype-dependent (control ≈100 a.u. → validated KO ≈5 a.u., ~95% loss, ~20-fold), whereas the 75 kDa band is genotype-invariant (≈95 → ≈93 a.u., ~2%, within typical quantification noise). The supported band-level inference is therefore: *the 50 kDa band is the candidate target band; the 75 kDa band is a non-specific/cross-reactive band or background.* This is a necessary but not sufficient validation claim, and it rests on assumptions that only orthogonal evidence can secure.

**1. Evidence register (what the figure actually is).** E1 is a summary bar plot of normalized band intensities from a synthetic knockout (KO) validation, with "equal loading" asserted in the title. Read-offs: 50 kDa, Control ≈100, KO ≈5; 75 kDa, Control ≈95, KO ≈93. Unreported parameters (flagged, not invented): replicate number n, variance/error bars, statistics, detection linearity and exposure, marker registration, KO construct design and epitope position, cell/tissue type, and whether "Validated KO" was independently genotyped. Because this is a quantification summary, it cannot show band morphology (doublets, smears), saturation, lane/transfer artifacts, or marker alignment — it must not be read as a raw blot.

**2. Evidence → inference → conclusion chain.**
- E1a: ~95% intensity loss at 50 kDa in the KO vs control.
- E1b: ~2% change at 75 kDa in the KO vs control.
- E1c: equal loading asserted (not demonstrated).
- I1 (from E1a + E1c): signal at 50 kDa tracks target genotype; the KO lane acts as an internal background reference, so genotype-attributable signal at 50 kDa ≈95 a.u. — the band is genetically specific.
- I2 (from E1b + E1c): genotype-attributable signal at 75 kDa ≈ control − KO ≈2 a.u. ≈ 0; a band that survives a true null cannot be the target epitope signal, so 75 kDa is cross-reactive/background (and usefully serves as an internal non-specific reference).
- C: the 50 kDa band is the supported target-candidate band; the 75 kDa band is excluded as target signal.

**3. Assumptions (explicit).** A1: the KO is a true null at the target locus *and only that locus* (no off-target edits, no neighboring-gene deletion, no clonal drift). A2: the null removes or destroys the epitope, with no stable truncation retaining the epitope that co-migrates at either mass. A3: quantification is within linear dynamic range (control 50 kDa at ≈100 a.u. could be saturated, which would compress, not create, the ratio). A4: "equal loading" is true; a global loading error is argued against by the unchanged 75 kDa band, but a mass-local transfer/artefact window is not excluded by a bar plot. A5: bars represent replicates with variance small relative to the effects (unreported; the 2% difference at 75 kDa is interpretable only against unknown noise). A6: loss of the 50 kDa band is direct (target protein absent), not indirect (loss of a partner). A7: sample identities/labels are correct ("Validated KO" is an assertion in E1, not evidence).

**4. Alternatives and how to discriminate them.**
- *Alt-1 (indirect loss):* the 50 kDa band is a cross-reactive protein lost secondarily — e.g., a binding partner destabilized by target loss, a neighboring gene co-deleted by the CRISPR lesion, or clonal selection in the KO line. Discriminators: immunoprecipitation–mass spectrometry (IP-MS) of the band; acute inducible KO/degron time course (rapid loss favours direct specificity); multiple independent KO clones; rescue (below). This is the most consequential alternative because it preserves E1a exactly.
- *Alt-2 (wrong band assignment):* 50 kDa is a proteolytic fragment and the full-length target runs elsewhere. If the full-length target were the 75 kDa band, the control−KO difference at 75 kDa would be positive and substantial; it is ≈0, so under A1–A3 the 75 kDa band carries no target-attributable signal. Overexpression and marker verification close this further.
- *Alt-3 (incomplete null):* the residual ~5 a.u. at 50 kDa is specific residual signal (hypomorph, mosaic WT contamination) rather than non-specific floor. This weakens but does not overturn I1; discriminate by genomic sequencing/qPCR of the locus on the same lysate, overexposure, and independent clones.
- *Alt-4 (quantification artefact):* saturation of the control 50 kDa band, or a mass-local transfer defect in the KO lane. Discriminators: raw blot images, total-protein (Ponceau/Stain-Free) loading control, replicate quantification in linear range.
- *Alt-5 (sample/label error):* a swap or mislabelled lane mimics specificity. Discriminator: genotyping the exact lysates used.

**5. Orthogonal evidence (all proposed experiments; none reported in E1).**
*Strengthening:* (O1) A second, epitope-independent antibody reproducing 50 kDa genotype-dependence and 75 kDa invariance. (O2) Rescue: re-expression of WT cDNA in the KO restores a 50 kDa band (optionally tag-shifted to separate exogenous from endogenous signal); dose-dependence of restoration is ideal. (O3) Knockdown titration (siRNA/shRNA): 50 kDa intensity tracks mRNA/protein level while 75 kDa stays flat. (O4) IP-MS or in-gel digest of the excised bands: target peptides identified at 50 kDa and absent in KO IP; the 75 kDa band identified as an off-target protein. (O5) Immunogen/peptide competition abolishing the 50 kDa band. (O6) Overexpression increasing the 50 kDa band (with expected mass/tag shift) but not 75 kDa. (O7) Multiple independent KO clones plus an acute inducible KO time course, excluding clonal adaptation (targets Alt-1, Alt-3). (O8) Reporting raw blots, total-protein loading controls, n, variance and statistics, converting I1/I2 from point estimates to testable effects.
*Overturning:* IP-MS identifying the 50 kDa band as a non-target protein; rescue of target expression failing to restore any 50 kDa signal; a verified null (genomic + mRNA) in which an epitope-independent antibody still detects 50 kDa; acute target depletion leaving 50 kDa unchanged; or demonstration that the KO lesion removes a neighbouring gene encoding the 50 kDa reactant. Any single one of these falsifies I1 and reassigns the 50 kDa band to Alt-1/Alt-5. Conversely, discovery that the "KO" is hypomorphic would downgrade, not reverse, the inference.

**6. Limits and uncertainty.** E1 supports only *genotype-dependence of quantified signal*, i.e. genetic specificity of a band — one pillar of antibody validation. It cannot establish band *identity* (requires MS/epitope mapping), exclude indirect loss (Alt-1), or quantify confidence (no n/variance). The summary-plot format additionally hides saturation, band shape and marker registration, so even the mass assignments (50/75 kDa) are provisional labels. Given E1 alone, the defensible statement is: "Under assumptions A1–A7, the 50 kDa band is the genotype-specific candidate target band and the 75 kDa band is non-specific; validation is provisional pending O1–O4."

**Recommended next action.** Treat 50 kDa as the working target band and 75 kDa as an internal non-specific reference to be excluded from quantification; prioritize O4 (IP-MS) and O2 (rescue) as the highest-information experiments, since they jointly discriminate the consequential Alt-1 from true target identity, and add O8 reporting so the ~95% vs ~2% contrast can be assessed against measured variance.
